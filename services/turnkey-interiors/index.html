Interior design services

Turnkey interiors
in Mohali & Tricity.

Turnkey interiors connect design and execution under an agreed project scope. DMS Studio offers this service for residential and commercial spaces in Mohali and the Tricity, beginning with the brief, site condition, selections and delivery responsibilities.

What the service involves

From the first idea to the final detail.

  • Written inclusions, exclusions and responsibilities
  • A design direction and coordinated selections
  • A project-specific programme after scope review
  • An approval process for materials and changes
  • Handover items and outstanding work recorded as agreed
Discuss your scope↗

One scope, clearly defined

The word turnkey is useful only when the inclusions are clear. Discuss drawings, material selection, procurement, site work, installation and styling as separate line items. Record what is supplied by the studio, by specialist contractors and by you.

Decisions in the right order

Layouts come before final joinery and electrical coordination. Major material and product selections should be approved before orders or installation depend on them. A shared record of decisions helps keep the work aligned when individual choices change.

A realistic programme and budget

The programme depends on the site, scale of work, access, approvals and procurement. Compare quotations against the same specification and inclusions. Keep a separate allowance for agreed changes or issues discovered on site; avoid choosing solely from an unexplained per-square-foot figure.

Useful answers

Questions, answered.

Does turnkey mean everything is automatically included?+

No. Appliances, loose furniture, civil work, specialist systems and decorative accessories may vary by proposal. The written scope is the reference for what is included.

Can you guarantee a completion date before reviewing my property?+

A meaningful programme requires the scope, site condition, selections and dependencies. Discuss a target date early, then confirm the project-specific schedule in the proposal.

How should changes be handled?+

Ask for changes to be documented with their cost and timing implications before approval. This keeps revised work separate from the original agreed scope.
