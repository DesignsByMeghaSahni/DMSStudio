Interior design services

Residential interiors
in Mohali & Tricity.

DMS Studio designs residential interiors in Mohali and across the Tricity. The service connects space planning, furniture, storage, finishes and lighting around the household’s daily needs. Design and execution responsibilities are defined in the agreed scope.

What the service involves

Homes, with a sense of you.

  • A defined room-by-room brief and scope
  • Space and furniture layouts appropriate to the agreed service
  • Concept, material and finish direction
  • Storage and lighting coordination within scope
  • Execution responsibilities and approvals agreed before work
Discuss your scope↗

A layout before a look

A home begins with the people using it. Share your daily routines, existing furniture and the rooms that need to work harder. We use that brief to discuss furniture placement, movement between spaces and the storage required before selecting a visual direction.

From one room to a whole home

A focused room brief needs clear boundaries: what stays, what changes and which services are affected. A complete home needs coordination between rooms so materials, woodwork and lighting feel connected. Tell us whether you need design support, execution or both.

What to prepare

Bring a measured plan if available, current photographs, your must-keep pieces and a working budget. For an occupied property, mention how you plan to live during the work. Finish samples should be considered alongside the room’s actual light and maintenance needs.

Useful answers

Questions, answered.

Can you work with furniture I already own?+

Yes. Share dimensions, photographs and the pieces you want to retain. Their scale, condition and position should be considered when developing the layout.

Is execution included in residential design?+

Execution is not assumed to be included in every design brief. Ask for a written proposal identifying design deliverables, supply, installation and any work handled separately.

Can a small home feel more spacious?+

Thoughtful circulation, appropriately sized furniture and purpose-built storage can improve how a small home works. Opera Home in the portfolio is a useful example of a compact residential brief.
