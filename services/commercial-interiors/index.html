Interior design services

Commercial interiors
in Mohali & Tricity.

DMS Studio provides commercial interior design for offices, salons and lifestyle spaces in Mohali and the Tricity. The brief brings customer experience, staff movement, furniture, lighting and the business’s visual identity into one layout.

What the service involves

A space that speaks for your business.

  • A functional brief based on visitors, team and operations
  • Space planning and furniture layouts
  • Material and lighting direction
  • Equipment and specialist-service coordination within scope
  • An execution plan tied to the agreed fit-out requirements
Discuss your scope↗

Start with how the business operates

Map customer arrival, waiting, service and departure alongside staff circulation and storage. Share equipment sizes, working positions and day-to-day maintenance needs. This turns a visual brief into a layout that can support the business.

Translate the brand into a space

Colour, material, proportion and light can express a business’s identity without relying on decoration everywhere. Review the key customer touchpoints and decide where the strongest visual moments belong. Essentia Salon in New Chandigarh demonstrates a documented commercial commission.

Coordinate the fit-out

Landlord conditions, building access, signage, equipment loads and specialist services need to be identified early. Relevant statutory approvals and specialist safety requirements must be confirmed for the particular use. The interior proposal should state the work and coordination it covers.

Useful answers

Questions, answered.

Do you design salon interiors?+

Yes. Essentia Salon in New Chandigarh is a documented DMS Studio project. Its portfolio page includes original photographs, custom furniture and lighting context.

Can you plan a small office?+

Share the team size, workstations, meeting needs, storage and visitor requirements. The suitability and scope can then be discussed around the actual space.

Are business approvals included?+

Do not assume they are. Identify the required permissions and specialist responsibilities with the relevant parties, and record any coordination included in the studio’s proposal.
