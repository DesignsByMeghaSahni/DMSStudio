A little clarity

Before we begin.

Where does DMS Studio work?+

We work across Mohali, Chandigarh, Panchkula, Zirakpur, Kharar and New Chandigarh.

Can you help with design and execution?+

DMS Studio offers turnkey interior services as well as space planning, modular kitchens, wardrobes, ceilings and lighting. The scope is agreed for each project.

What should I bring to the first conversation?+

A floor plan or a few photographs, the property location, your priorities and an indication of budget and timing are a useful starting point.

Do you work with clients based overseas?+

Opera Home was coordinated remotely for an NRI client. Contact the studio to discuss whether remote coordination is suitable for your project.
