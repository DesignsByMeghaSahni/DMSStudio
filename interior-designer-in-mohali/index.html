Planning a complete home in Mohali

Begin with the rooms you use most

For a family home, start with circulation between the entrance, kitchen, living area and bedrooms. Decide where shoes, daily bags, cleaning supplies and seasonal items will live before finalising decorative wall treatments. A floor plan with furniture dimensions helps identify these needs early.

Coordinate woodwork, ceilings and lighting

Babbar Residency brings kitchen planning, woodwork and gypsum ceilings together in one project. Its mix of warm timber and expressive colour is a useful reference for discussing how a coordinated home can still give individual rooms their own character.

A compact apartment needs a clear hierarchy

Opera Home shows a different brief: a 1BHK bohemian interior with smart storage, coordinated remotely for an NRI client. Use this project to discuss priorities for a smaller footprint, rather than copying a larger-home layout into fewer square feet.
