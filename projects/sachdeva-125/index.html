Studio
DMS Studio · Mohali

A 400-gaj kothi in Sector 125, Mohali, planned as a welcoming family home. The project brings together expansive living and dining areas, a feature media wall, a dedicated mandir, kitchen storage and two bedrooms with distinct colour palettes.

Design concept

The home balances a formal, softly illuminated living and dining setting with personal bedroom schemes: one in deep blue and another in warm rose. A detailed media wall and dedicated mandir give the shared spaces clear focal points, while built-in cabinetry supports everyday storage.

Scope

Residential interior design · Living and dining areas · Feature media wall · Mandir detailing · Kitchen and storage planning · Bedroom interiors

Materials & finishes +

The project photographs show decorative wall panelling, built-in cabinetry, upholstered bedroom features, integrated lighting and stone-look flooring. Exact product brands, material specifications and construction scope were not supplied.
