This 3BHK apartment at TDI Wellington brings together an open living and dining area, a connected modular kitchen, thoughtfully planned bedrooms and warm layered lighting. Soft neutrals, wood-finish surfaces and restrained accents give the family spaces a calm, cohesive feel.

Design concept

The interior balances practical family living with a warm, contemporary palette. Curved lounge seating softens the living area, while timber detailing and carefully layered ceiling lighting add depth. The connected kitchen, dining and lounge spaces read as one welcoming home.

Scope

Living and dining interiors · Modular kitchen · Bedroom interiors and storage · Media wall · Ceiling and lighting details

Materials & finishes +

Neutral upholstery, wood-finish surfaces, grey kitchen cabinetry and layered ceiling and task lighting. Specific brands and product specifications were not provided.
