Sheena Nail Studio in Phase 3B2, Mohali, brings nail treatment stations, arched product displays and a comfortable guest lounge together in a bright, welcoming salon interior.

Design concept

The photographed interior pairs a soft neutral palette with warm accent lighting, arched shelves for nail polish, a floral lounge feature and clear views across the treatment area. The result feels calm and personable while keeping the salon’s services and product displays easy to see.

Scope

Commercial salon interiors · Nail treatment area · Guest lounge · Product display and lighting

Materials & finishes +

The photographs show pale stone-look flooring, neutral upholstered seating, arched illuminated product shelves, warm wall lights and a floral feature mural. Specific product brands and finish schedules were not supplied.
