Prince Residence

A 250-gaj kothi where a sculpted media wall, custom storage and a considered kitchen bring warmth to everyday living.

A closer look.

Prince Residence is a 250-gaj kothi in Sector 123, Mohali. The project photographs show a planned modular kitchen, custom wardrobes and storage, a statement living-room media wall, decorative doors and layered ceiling lighting.

Design concept

The home brings together warm wood tones, pale cabinetry and expressive stone-look feature finishes. Integrated storage keeps the kitchen and bedrooms considered, while the living-room media wall and ceiling details give the main spaces a distinct focal point.

Scope

250-gaj residential interiors · Kitchen cabinetry · Living-room media wall · Wardrobes and storage · Doors · Ceiling and lighting details

Materials & finishes +

The photographs show pale kitchen cabinetry, dark wood finishes, stone-look media-wall cladding, decorative wood doors, tiled floors and integrated ceiling lighting. Specific product brands and finish schedules were not supplied.
