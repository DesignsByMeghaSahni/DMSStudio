Ruchit Residence is a 400-gaj kothi in Phase 1, Mohali. Project photographs document its contemporary front elevation, screened balcony, geometric exterior feature wall, stair and landing, living-room panelling, built-in storage and puja alcove.

Design concept

The residence pairs a crisp pale facade with dark metal screens, warm timber-look soffits and a textured exterior feature wall. Inside, the photographs show a restrained palette across the living area, circulation spaces, storage and a dedicated puja alcove. Some images document the property during fit-out and are identified accordingly.

Scope

400-gaj private residence · Exterior elevation · Balcony screens · Living-room panelling · Stair and landing · Built-in storage · Puja alcove

Materials & finishes +

Visible finishes include pale exterior cladding, geometric textured panels, dark metal screens, timber-look soffit detailing, marble-look flooring, wall panelling and built-in cabinetry. Product brands and technical specifications were not supplied.
