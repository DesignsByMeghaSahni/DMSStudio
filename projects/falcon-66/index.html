This 3BHK home at Falcon Society in Sector 66, Mohali brings the living and dining areas, bedrooms, a dedicated puja space and integrated display storage into one considered home story.

Design concept

The supplied photographs show a softly lit, neutral-toned home with a dedicated mandir alcove, built-in display shelving, and coordinated living, dining and bedroom spaces. The design brings everyday function and a calm visual rhythm together across the 3BHK.

Scope

3BHK residential interiors · Living and dining areas · Puja-room detailing · Bedroom and storage planning

Materials & finishes +

The photographs show light-toned cabinetry and wall finishes, decorative shelving, integrated lighting and a dedicated puja alcove. Exact product brands and finish specifications were not supplied.
