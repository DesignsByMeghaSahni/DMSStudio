Planning a Modular Kitchen in Mohali: A Checklist Before You Choose Finishes

Before comparing shutters and colours, map how you cook, what needs to be stored and where services sit. This checklist helps Mohali homeowners have a more useful first kitchen-design conversation.

By DMS StudioPublished 2026-11-23Practical design guidance
Quick answer

Before choosing modular-kitchen finishes, record the room dimensions, plumbing and electrical points, appliance sizes, cooking habits and storage needs. Then review the work triangle, clearances, ventilation and access to services with a designer or relevant professional. Compare finishes only after the layout and scope are clear. A final plan must be checked against the actual site; photographs and generic layouts cannot confirm measurements or service conditions.

Write down how the kitchen is used

List the routines that happen most days: who cooks, whether two people work at once, which appliances stay on the counter, and how groceries are stored. Note the items that are awkward to reach or clean in the current kitchen. This short brief gives the layout a real job to do and prevents the conversation from starting with colour samples alone.

Measure the room and services

Record wall lengths, ceiling height, doors, windows, columns and the positions of water, drainage, gas, power and ventilation. These are starting measurements only; a site check is needed before drawings and installation. Confirm appliance models or at least their required sizes, since a fridge, oven or dishwasher can change cabinet dimensions and clearances. Do not cover access points or assume that a service can be moved without checking the relevant requirements.

Plan work zones and storage

Think about the path from refrigerator to sink to cooking area, the counter space needed beside each appliance, and where dishes, dry goods and cleaning supplies belong. Keep frequently used items reachable and consider how drawers and shutters open next to one another. The best storage plan depends on the household’s cookware and habits, not just the number of cabinets.

Choose finishes after the plan

Shutter material, countertop, hardware and backsplash should be compared for cleaning, wear, light and budget scope. A glossy finish may reflect more light but can show fingerprints; a textured surface may need different care. Ask what is included in each specification and how edges, handles and corners will be finished. Colour swatches should be reviewed in the kitchen’s own daylight and under its evening lighting.

Coordinate the details before installation

Confirm the drawing, appliance schedule, electrical points, plumbing, lighting and ventilation before fabrication. Identify who is responsible for each part of the work and what must be ready before cabinets arrive. Your project sequence will depend on the property and scope. Questions to take into a kitchen consultation

Bring a floor plan if available, current photographs, appliance information, a list of storage frustrations and a rough project timeline. Ask for the scope, materials, installation responsibilities and exclusions to be explained in writing. A site-based quotation is more useful than a generic online rate because the room, services and specification differ from home to home.

Useful answers

Common questions

What should I decide first in a modular kitchen?+

Start with how you cook, measured site conditions, appliances, services and storage. Select finishes after the layout and scope are understood.

Which is better for a kitchen, acrylic or laminate?+

Neither is best for every home. Compare appearance, cleaning, wear, edge treatment, cost and the quality of installation for the exact product being offered.

Can I change plumbing or electrical points during a kitchen project?+

Possibly, but the existing site and applicable requirements need checking. Coordinate changes with qualified trades before fabrication and installation.
