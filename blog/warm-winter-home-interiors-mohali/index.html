How to Make a Mohali Home Feel Warmer in Winter

A more comfortable winter room often starts with small adjustments to light, textiles and furniture placement. Here is a practical way to make the home feel cosier without a major renovation.

By DMS StudioPublished 2026-11-09Practical design guidance
Quick answer

To make a home feel warmer in winter, first improve comfort rather than adding seasonal decor: check drafts and window coverings, add washable throws or rugs where useful, use warm-toned lamps for evening light, and keep furniture clear of heaters and walking routes. Choose textiles that suit the household and are easy to maintain. If you are considering insulation, electrical or building changes, get site-specific advice before work begins.

Begin with the room at the time you use it

Notice when the room feels uncomfortable. Is the issue a chilly floor in the morning, glare from a low winter sun, or a living room that feels flat after dark? Each problem points to a different response. A rug may make a seating area feel more comfortable underfoot; curtains may help manage light and privacy; an additional lamp can make evening reading easier. Note the room’s orientation and where people actually sit before buying anything.

Use soft layers with a clear purpose

A throw, cushion cover or rug can add softness and a seasonal accent, but the layers should still work for everyday life. Choose fabrics that can be cleaned in the way your family needs. In a busy living room, a washable cover may be more useful than a delicate material. Repeat one or two colours already present in the room and leave some surfaces clear; a coordinated edit feels calmer than a pile of unrelated seasonal objects.

Make evening light feel comfortable

A single bright ceiling fitting can make a room feel stark once daylight fades. Add light where it is useful: a lamp beside a reading chair, a soft source near the sofa, and general light for moving around. Avoid glare on television screens and keep electrical cords away from walkways. Permanent fittings and wiring should be planned with a qualified electrician. Our <a href="../../blog/the-art-of-layered-light/index.html">layered lighting guide</a> explains how multiple light sources support different activities.

Arrange furniture around comfort and movement

Move seating away from a drafty window or door if the room allows, while keeping a comfortable route through the space. Check that curtains can open fully and that vents, radiators or other heating equipment are not blocked. Do not place fabric close to heat sources. In a smaller flat, a lighter side table or flexible stool can add function without crowding the main path.

Choose changes that suit the home

A winter refresh does not require a new palette for the whole house. Test one change first, such as a warmer lamp, a heavier curtain lining or a rug under the seating group. If the home has persistent damp, drafts or heating issues, decorative changes may not address the cause; seek the appropriate building or maintenance professional. A short winter comfort checklist

Walk through the room at the time you use it. Check the window coverings, evening light, seating distance and clear walking route. Add one useful soft layer, test the colour in daylight and after dark, and keep heat sources and cables unobstructed. If the same discomfort remains, investigate its cause before spending on decorative fixes.

Useful answers

Common questions

How can I make my living room cosy without renovating?+

Adjust the seating layout, add a useful rug or washable textile, use warm comfortable lighting and clear visual clutter. Start with one change and see how it works in the room.

Which curtain fabric is best for winter?+

It depends on the window, privacy needs, daylight and maintenance. Consider lined or heavier curtains where appropriate, but make sure they fit and operate safely around heaters and doors.

Do warm lights make a room warmer?+

They change the visual feel of a room, but do not replace safe, adequate heating or building repairs. Choose lighting for comfort and activity, and address drafts separately.
