Home planning

How to plan a 3BHK interior in Mohali before choosing finishes

A 3BHK home is easier to plan when the family’s routines, storage and circulation are resolved before selecting finishes. This sequence can help organise an early design brief.

By DMS StudioPublished 2026-10-15Updated 2026-10-15

Editorial photograph · Unsplash. Not a DMS project.

Quick answer

For a 3BHK interior, first confirm the measured plan and site conditions. Then plan shared-room circulation, kitchen workflow, bedroom storage, lighting and the budgeted scope before finalising materials or decorative details.

Start with a verified plan

Use the builder or existing plan as a reference, then check dimensions, columns, doors, windows, service points and ceiling conditions at the property before detailed decisions. Mark which information is confirmed and which needs a site visit. This is particularly useful in an apartment, where plumbing, electrical points and building rules may constrain changes. Do not treat a marketing plan or an approximate sketch as a fabrication drawing.

Give the shared rooms a clear sequence

Map the route from entry to living, dining, kitchen and bedrooms. Note where household members cross paths, where visitors sit and where daily items accumulate. A living room may need to serve family time and guests; the dining area may also become a work surface. Test furniture layouts on paper before adding partitions or feature elements. Clear walking space often makes a room feel more generous than extra furniture does.

Plan each bedroom for its user

A bedroom needs more than a bed and wardrobe. Ask who uses it, what should be stored, whether a study or dressing area is required and what furniture will stay. Check wardrobe door movement, bedside access, daylight, curtains and charging points together. A child’s room may need a layout that can adapt as interests and study needs change. A guest room may require flexible storage. These uses should be decided before finalising joinery and finishes.

Coordinate kitchen, lighting and storage

Confirm appliance dimensions and the preparation, washing, cooking and storage sequence before cabinet sizes are finalised. Check how drawers and doors open beside each other. Plan task lighting where work happens, general light for movement and switches that are convenient on arrival. Measure storage needs instead of estimating cabinet volume from a photograph. Final services and electrical work should be reviewed for the specific property by qualified professionals.

Separate scope from styling

Write down what the project includes: planning, drawings, kitchen or wardrobe work, ceiling and lighting, finishes, procurement, installation and any specialist services. Agree a working budget and note exclusions, taxes, delivery, appliances and site preparation. Once these are clear, select a palette that supports the layout and the home’s daylight. A calm material direction can connect rooms without making every room identical.

Project context from Mohali

TDI Wellington is a documented 3BHK apartment project in Sector 117, Mohali. Its portfolio page shows the living and dining area, connected kitchen and other rooms using supplied project imagery. Treat the photographs as evidence of that project’s visible decisions, not as a plan for a different apartment. The dimensions, building rules, family routines and service points of your own home should shape its design.

What should be planned first in a 3BHK interior?

Confirm the site measurements and building constraints first. Then plan circulation, room use, storage, furniture and kitchen workflow. Coordinate lighting and services with that layout before choosing final finishes and decorative details.

Useful answers

Common questions

Can a 3BHK plan be prepared before possession?+

Early planning can start from available drawings and a design brief, but measurements and site conditions should be checked after access is available and before detailed execution decisions.

Should all three bedrooms use the same design?+

They can share a coherent overall palette while responding to different users, storage needs, daylight and activities.

Does a project photograph show the exact materials and dimensions?+

It documents what is visible in a particular project. Confirm specifications and dimensions for your own property rather than inferring them from a photograph.
