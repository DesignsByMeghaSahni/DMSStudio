3BHK Interior Quotation Checklist for Mohali Homeowners

Two interior quotations are difficult to compare when their scope, materials and responsibilities differ. Use this checklist to understand what is included before choosing a plan for your home.

By DMS StudioPublished 2026-12-21Practical design guidance
Quick answer

A useful 3BHK interior quotation should describe the rooms and work included, materials and finish specifications, quantities or drawings where applicable, execution responsibilities, exclusions, payment stages, dependencies and how changes are handled. There is no single reliable price for every home: site condition, design scope, material choices and execution requirements vary. Ask for a site-based discussion and a written scope before comparing totals.

Why a single total can be misleading

A headline amount does not explain which rooms, carpentry, finishes, electrical work, lighting, furniture or site coordination are covered. Two proposals may use different materials or quantities, or one may exclude work that the other includes. Compare the scope line by line and ask what is assumed. Do not choose from a per-square-foot figure without understanding what that measure includes and how it applies to your property.

Check room-by-room inclusions

List the spaces in your 3BHK and the work expected in each: kitchen, wardrobes, bedrooms, living areas, ceilings, lighting, bathrooms or painting. Confirm whether loose furniture, appliances, soft furnishings, demolition, disposal and repairs are included or excluded. A clear schedule makes it easier to see where proposals differ and whether the design meets the household’s priorities.

Ask for material details

Names such as premium, luxury or standard are not enough to identify a specification. Ask for the product or material type, thickness or grade where relevant, finish, hardware, edge treatment and warranty information. Confirm what alternatives are allowed and how a change affects the scope. Review samples where possible and check that the specified finish suits the way the home is used.

Understand execution and payment responsibilities

Clarify who coordinates trades, measures the site, protects existing areas, manages deliveries and checks the work. Ask how site access, approvals, service changes and delays are handled. Payment stages should be tied to a written agreement and clear deliverables. Get changes approved in writing so both sides understand the revised scope and schedule.

Allow for site-specific factors

The condition of the property, existing electrical and plumbing services, access, lift or society rules, demolition and material availability can affect the plan. A final proposal needs a site review and detailed brief. If you are comparing estimates, give each provider the same information and ask them to identify assumptions and exclusions.

What to prepare before requesting a quote

Share the property location, floor plan or measurements, current photographs, rooms to include, priorities, preferred timing and any items to keep. Useful answers

Common questions

How much does a 3BHK interior cost in Mohali?+

There is no single figure that fits every 3BHK. The scope, site condition, materials and execution requirements vary. Contact the studio with your plan and priorities for a project-specific discussion.

What should an interior design quotation include?+

It should clearly state the rooms and work covered, specifications, exclusions, responsibilities, payment stages, dependencies and how changes are approved.

Should I compare interior design quotes by square foot?+

Use any area-based number only after confirming what it includes. Room-by-room scope and written specifications make proposals more meaningful to compare.

Can I get an exact quote from photos alone?+

Photos can support an initial conversation, but accurate scope and costing usually require measurements, a site review and decisions about materials and work.
