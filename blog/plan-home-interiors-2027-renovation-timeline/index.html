Planning Home Interiors for 2027: A Realistic Renovation Timeline

A home project moves through decisions, drawings, approvals, procurement and site work. Use this planning sequence to prepare for a conversation about your 2027 interior project.

By DMS StudioPublished 2026-12-28Practical design guidance
Quick answer

Start a home interior project by defining the rooms, priorities, budget range and target date, then arrange a site review and design brief. Allow time for layouts, approvals, material selections, detailed specifications, procurement and execution. The total programme depends on the home, scope, decisions, approvals and material availability, so agree a realistic schedule with the project team before making commitments.

Step 1: Set the brief and constraints

List who uses the home, what is not working, which furniture should stay and what must change. Note a target move-in or completion date, but also any fixed constraints such as school schedules, society rules, work-from-home needs or access to the property. Decide which rooms matter most if the work needs to be phased.

See the documented stages and spaces in the Falcon 66 Residence project. For more project updates, follow DMS Studio on Instagram.

Step 2: Survey and plan the layout

Share available floor plans and photographs, then verify dimensions and site conditions before design decisions depend on them. Review circulation, storage, furniture sizes, natural light and service locations. Identify questions about plumbing, electrical work, approvals or structural conditions for the right professional to review. Early checks reduce the chance of designing around assumptions.

Step 3: Agree the concept and scope

Once the layout works, develop a material and lighting direction that suits the household and property. Confirm which rooms and trades are included, what the drawings will specify and who will coordinate execution. A written scope with exclusions is easier to schedule and compare than a general promise to make the home beautiful.

Step 4: Finalise selections and procurement

Confirm product specifications, quantities, colours, hardware, appliance sizes and delivery responsibilities before orders are placed. Some items may have longer lead times, and alternatives can affect the design. Keep a record of approvals and changes so the design team, vendors and household are working from the same information.

Step 5: Coordinate the site work

Execution may involve preparation, electrical or plumbing coordination, carpentry, finishes, installation and checks. The order depends on the specific project. Confirm access, working hours, building rules, protection of existing areas and who handles each trade. Keep time for inspections and corrections rather than planning every day as if nothing can change.

What can change the timeline?

Site discoveries, approvals, late design decisions, product availability, delivery access and changes to scope can all affect a programme. A designer should discuss these dependencies openly, but no general article can guarantee a finish date. The <a href="../../projects/falcon-66/index.html">Falcon 66 residence</a> and other <a href="../../projects.html">DMS Studio projects</a> illustrate different homes; your plan needs to be based on your own property and brief.

Get ready for a useful first meeting

Bring the location, floor plan if available, current photographs, room priorities, items to retain, a budget range and the date you hope to use the space. Useful answers

Common questions

How long does a full home interior project take?+

There is no universal timeline. It depends on the property, agreed scope, design decisions, approvals, materials, procurement and execution. A project schedule should be discussed after those details are reviewed.

When should I contact an interior designer for a 2027 move?+

Start once you know the property and approximate target date, especially if layouts, approvals or custom items are involved. More lead time gives room for decisions and procurement.

Can a home renovation be completed in phases?+

Often it can be planned in stages, but access, services, dust, safety and material continuity need consideration. Agree which spaces remain usable and how phases connect.

What should I prepare before a design consultation?+

Bring a floor plan or measurements, current photos, the property location, priorities, items to retain, inspiration references, budget range and target timing.
