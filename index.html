Useful answers

Helpful answers

What does DMS Studio design?+

DMS Studio plans residential interiors and commercial spaces, including homes, apartments and salons. The proposed work is defined for each property.

Where is DMS Studio located?+

DMS Studio is based in Sunny Enclave, Mohali, and serves Mohali, Chandigarh, Panchkula, Zirakpur, Kharar and New Chandigarh.

What should I share to start a project conversation?+

Share the property location, a plan or current photos if available, the rooms you want to address and your main priorities. The studio can then discuss a useful next step.

Can I see completed DMS Studio work?+

Yes. The portfolio includes individual project pages with photographs, locations and project details for residential and commercial spaces.
