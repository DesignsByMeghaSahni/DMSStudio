A practical brief for your Kharar home

Start with storage, not a catalogue

List what needs to be stored in each room, including luggage, cleaning equipment and occasional-use items. Decide which items should be visible and which should be behind doors. This gives wardrobe and cabinet layouts a purpose before choosing a laminate.

Check the property before committing to finishes

Record the condition of walls, services and window openings during the site review. Discuss any signs of dampness or service problems before covering surfaces. Decorative work should follow an agreed plan for resolving underlying issues.

Make kitchen decisions together

Appliance sizes, countertop height, sockets, plumbing and cabinet openings affect one another. Bring appliance details to the layout conversation so the kitchen can be coordinated as a whole. A realistic brief distinguishes essential fittings from optional accessories.
