From possession to a usable home

Compare the handover plan with the site

Check dimensions, column positions, doors and service points before finalising furniture or cabinetry. Record the items the builder is handing over and the items your interior scope must supply. This helps prevent gaps and duplicate work.

Confirm building access before scheduling

Ask the building team about work hours, lift use, delivery access, debris removal and any fit-out permissions. These are property-specific conditions to confirm, not assumptions a design proposal should make. Share them with the studio early.

Choose adaptable furniture around fixed storage

In a compact apartment, deep joinery and oversized seating can reduce usable circulation. Begin with the largest fixed elements, then check dining-chair pull-out, wardrobe openings and the route through the room. Prioritise what supports daily use.

Useful answers

Questions, answered.

Can you help with interiors after apartment possession in Zirakpur?+

Yes. Share the handover status, plan and desired scope. Measurements and existing services should be checked before approving the final interior layout.

How long will an apartment interior take?+

There is no reliable single duration for every home. Scope, site condition, selections, approvals, procurement and access affect the schedule. Ask for a project-specific programme after these are defined.

Can we begin while living outside India?+

Opera Home provides a documented example of remote coordination for an NRI client. Contact the studio to discuss communication, approvals and local site access for your own project.
