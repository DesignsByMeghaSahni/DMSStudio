A clear identity for spaces people return to.

Designing around the visitor experience

Map the experience from the entrance

For a salon, studio or office, identify where visitors arrive, wait, meet staff and receive a service. Keep staff movement, stock and cleaning needs in the same plan. An attractive reception should also help the business operate clearly.

Make light serve the work

A commercial scheme needs to balance atmosphere with the visibility required for its tasks. Discuss equipment, working positions, mirrors and screen locations before lighting layouts are fixed. Electrical requirements should be coordinated with qualified professionals.

Connect identity to practical finishes

The material palette should reflect the business without ignoring maintenance. Review sample finishes, cleaning expectations and the areas likely to receive frequent use. Essentia Salon is a starting point for discussing this balance, not a template every business should repeat.
