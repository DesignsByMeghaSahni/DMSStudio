## Replace a photograph
Keep the basename to retain every reference. Replace original and regenerate variants at 640/1280/1920 maximum widths with `scripts/optimise-assets.py` (Pillow required only for this optional maintainer step). Then run `python3 scripts/build.py` to update intrinsic dimensions and srcsets. Alternatively export those WebP files manually, update `assets/image-dimensions.json` and regenerate the HTML. Do not upscale small originals. Update project alt text and this register.
