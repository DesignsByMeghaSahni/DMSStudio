Thinking through a Chandigarh renovation

Document what already works

For an existing home, separate the features worth retaining from the changes you need. Photograph storage, electrical points and areas showing wear. A measured survey is more reliable than assuming an old drawing matches the property today.

Make alterations a separate decision

Aesthetic changes, service changes and structural alterations require different checks. Discuss proposed wall changes and any relevant property or building approvals before execution. Interior concepts do not replace advice from a qualified structural professional where it is needed.

Plan for life during the work

If you intend to remain at home, discuss room-by-room access, dust protection, temporary storage and the availability of a kitchen or bathroom. The practical sequence of work can matter as much as the final palette.
