Choosing your designer

Start with the right people, the right brief and a clear understanding of the work.

How do I choose the best interior designer for my home in Mohali or Chandigarh?

+

Choose by relevant completed work, clarity of scope and how well the designer understands your household. Ask to see a project comparable to your flat or kothi, discuss who handles site decisions and compare written proposals. A beautiful portfolio matters, but so do usable storage, reliable communication and clearly specified materials.

What should I ask an interior designer before hiring them?

+

Ask what drawings you receive, how revisions work, who purchases materials and who checks installation. Discuss exclusions, payment stages, approvals and handover documents before you commit. Bring your floor plan and a short list of problems you want the design to solve so the conversation goes beyond colours and furniture.

Link to this answer ↗

Do I need an architect, an interior designer or both?

+

An architect focuses on the building and its overall planning; an interior designer develops the way its rooms function and feel. New construction or substantial building changes can require both disciplines, with engineering input where needed. Tell DMS whether you need interior work, an elevation or building planning so responsibilities can be clarified before appointment.

Link to this answer ↗

What is the difference between an interior designer and a decorator?

+

Interior design can involve layouts, built-in furniture, lighting coordination and detailed drawings. Decoration concentrates more on furnishings, artwork, colour and accessories within an existing space. If your kitchen workflow, electrical points or storage need changing, describe those needs early rather than asking only for a new look.

Link to this answer ↗

Should I hire a designer or work directly with a carpenter?

+

A carpenter can execute clearly specified joinery. A designer helps coordinate that joinery with movement, lighting, furniture and the rest of the home. For a straightforward replacement cupboard, a carpenter may be sufficient; for several connected rooms, design coordination can reduce conflicting decisions. Compare responsibilities as well as the amount quoted.

Link to this answer ↗

How can I tell whether a portfolio shows real completed projects?

+

Look for several views of the same room, close-up details, project context and walkthrough footage where available. Ask which images are photographs, visualisations or edited photographs. DMS project pages identify the supplied work, while the homepage Opera Home interactive scene is labelled as an AI visualisation rather than a captured property tour.

Process, timelines & turnkey work

Understand what happens between the first conversation and moving in.

What should a turnkey interior package actually include?

+

It should define the design work, supply and installation being undertaken, with a list of exclusions. Drawings, finish selections, joinery, site coordination and handover may form part of the scope, but the word turnkey does not specify them automatically. Ask who is responsible for each trade and how changes will be approved.

Can I get design drawings and appoint my own contractors?

+

Yes, design-only work with your own contractors can be discussed. Confirm which drawings, specifications, revisions and site support DMS would provide, and who will be responsible for procurement and installation.

How early should I approach an interior designer before possession?

+

Start once you have a reasonably reliable floor plan and an expected possession window. This gives you time to establish room priorities and a design direction. Final measurements and site readiness still need verification before production; a builder’s brochure plan should not be treated as a fabrication drawing.

How are design changes and material substitutions handled?

+

Record the proposed change, the reason for it and any impact on cost or completion before approving it. Luxury homes & personal style

A refined home should feel as good to live in as it looks.

What makes a home feel luxurious without looking overdecorated?

+

Start with generous movement, furniture that fits and a coherent material palette. Well-resolved junctions, comfortable lighting and useful concealed storage often make a stronger impression than adding more feature walls. Choose a few details to emphasise and allow quieter surfaces around them so the room feels composed rather than crowded.

Link to this answer ↗

Can I achieve a premium look within a controlled budget?

+

Yes, by concentrating attention where you experience it every day. A well-proportioned sofa, thoughtful curtains, a restrained finish palette and properly placed lighting can work together without every surface becoming a statement. Retain sound elements where possible and compare practical alternatives before committing to imported or highly customised pieces.

Link to this answer ↗

How do I choose between modern, contemporary and classic interiors?

+

Collect references you enjoy, then describe what attracts you: clean lines, warmth, symmetry, texture or colour. Your home can combine those qualities without copying a complete style label. Test the direction against daylight, existing architecture and your furniture preferences before finalising decorative details.

Link to this answer ↗

Are imported materials essential for luxury interiors?

+

No. Materials should be selected for their appearance, suitability, detailing and support. A locally available finish installed carefully can work beautifully, while an imported product may create maintenance or replacement challenges. Compare samples and technical information, and choose the material that suits the design and the way you will use it.

Link to this answer ↗

How can I make a luxury living room practical for family use?

+

Plan seating around conversation and everyday movement, then consider children, guests, pets and cleaning. Check fabric care requirements, table edges, charging points and where everyday objects will go. A formal appearance is easier to maintain when storage and daily routines have been designed into the room from the beginning.

Can you use my Instagram or Pinterest inspiration without copying it?

+

Share references and explain which details appeal to you. DMS can discuss how that mood relates to your room dimensions, light, existing possessions and priorities. Use references as a starting brief; the final combination should respond to your home rather than reproduce another designer’s entire room.

Room planning, kitchens & materials

Make the rooms you use most work better every day.

What should I plan first in a 3BHK apartment?

+

Start with who uses each room and what needs to fit there. Decide sleeping arrangements, guest use, working from home and storage before choosing wall finishes. Test circulation and furniture sizes on the plan, then coordinate lighting and electrical requirements around that layout. Falcon 66 and TDI Wellington offer relevant DMS apartment examples.

Should I choose a modular kitchen or a carpenter-built kitchen?

+

Neither a modular nor a carpenter-built kitchen is automatically better. Modular units can provide a defined production system; site-built work may address unusual dimensions. Compare the layout, material specification, moisture detailing, hardware, installation and access for future repairs.

Which is better for kitchen shutters: laminate, acrylic or PU?

+

There is no single best finish for every kitchen. Compare appearance, edge detailing, cleaning, repair options and how marks show on the selected colour. Review full samples under your kitchen lighting and ask for the specific product’s care instructions. The underlying board, hinges and workmanship matter alongside the visible surface.

Link to this answer ↗

How do I add storage without making a small flat feel crowded?

+

List what needs to be stored and where it is used, then design storage to match those objects. Concentrate larger cabinets on suitable walls and leave comfortable routes around furniture. Combine concealed everyday storage with a limited amount of open display, rather than filling every spare wall with cupboards.

Link to this answer ↗

Is a false ceiling necessary for good lighting?

+

No. A lighting plan can use surface-mounted fittings, pendants, wall lights and joinery lighting where appropriate. Decide what each area needs before choosing the ceiling treatment. If a lowered ceiling is proposed, consider room height, service access and how the lighting will be maintained.

How do I design a child’s themed bedroom that will still work as they grow?

+

Keep the long-lasting elements flexible: useful storage, an appropriately planned study area and furniture that suits the room. Put changeable themes into artwork, bedding and selected finishes. Evaan Joshi’s football-themed bedroom in the DMS portfolio shows how a child’s interest can establish the room’s identity.

How can I include a mandir in a modern home?

+

Choose a calm, accessible position and plan the platform, storage and lighting around how your family uses it. Discuss the objects you want to display, cleaning needs and any ritual requirements before fixing dimensions. A mandir can belong to the home’s material palette while keeping its own distinct character.

Renovation & building coordination

Resolve the existing conditions before investing in the visible finishes.

Can an older home in Chandigarh or Panchkula be renovated in stages?

+

A phased renovation may be practical if the work can be separated safely and services remain usable. Begin by mapping shared electrical, plumbing and flooring work so a finished room is not reopened later. Discuss access, dust control, temporary facilities and whether you can reasonably stay in the property during each stage.

Link to this answer ↗

Can I change walls or combine rooms during an interior renovation?

+

A visual layout proposal is not permission to remove a wall. Have the existing structure and services assessed by the appropriate professionals before planning demolition. Building or society permissions may also be relevant. Tell the design team about previous alterations and obtain a clear technical plan before any changes begin.

Link to this answer ↗

Can the exterior elevation and interiors be designed together?

+

Yes, exterior elevation and interiors can be designed together. Coordinate windows, materials, lighting and indoor-outdoor transitions. Confirm separate responsibilities for architectural design, structural work, approvals and interiors before work starts.

Link to this answer ↗

What should I check before starting interiors in a newly handed-over flat?

+

Record defects, verify dimensions and inspect the condition of walls, floors and service points before covering them with finishes. Confirm access, working hours and material delivery arrangements with the society. Resolve builder-related issues early where possible and keep the final measured plan separate from the original sales drawing.

Link to this answer ↗

Can Vastu preferences be included in a practical interior layout?

+

Share your specific preferences at the briefing stage so they can be considered alongside daylight, circulation and existing services. If you use a Vastu adviser, involve them before the layout is frozen. Agree which requirements are essential and where flexibility is possible rather than changing completed work later.

Link to this answer ↗

How should I plan interiors for Mohali’s summer heat and everyday dust?

+

Look at window orientation, shading, ventilation and how each room is used before selecting finishes. Discuss curtain layers, accessible cleaning details and materials whose care requirements fit your routine. Interior decoration cannot resolve every building comfort issue, so coordinate cooling and envelope concerns with the relevant specialists when needed.

Salons, commercial spaces & handover

Design for the customer experience and the people who use the space every day.

How is salon interior design different from residential design?

+

A salon needs a clear customer journey and work areas suited to the services offered. Reception, waiting, styling, washing, storage and staff movement should be planned together. Materials and lighting need to support frequent use and cleaning. Essentia Salon in New Chandigarh provides a completed DMS commercial example.

What should I consider when designing a nail salon in Mohali?

+

Start with the treatment menu, staff workflow and the number of stations the premises can comfortably accommodate. Plan product storage, customer seating, lighting and cleaning arrangements together. Ventilation and relevant operating requirements need specialist attention. Sheena Nail Studio in Phase 3B2 offers a project reference for discussing the design direction.

Can a compact office look premium without wasting floor space?

+

Yes. Define what clients and staff need first: reception, private conversations, working areas and storage. Use furniture scaled to the space and keep movement routes clear. A coordinated palette and considered lighting can create a confident identity without oversized desks or decorative partitions that reduce useful area.

What should be checked before accepting an interior handover?

+

Walk through the agreed scope with the team and record unfinished items or defects. Check doors, drawers, visible finishes and installed fittings, then request relevant manuals, invoices and care information. Keep a dated snag list with agreed responsibilities so outstanding work is easier to follow up.
