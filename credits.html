Original DMS projects

Babbar Residency, Opera Home, Essentia Salon, Yogesh Residence, TDI Wellington, Sheena Nail Studio and Prince Residence photographs are from DMS Studio project imagery supplied or authorised for this site. The Sheena Nail Studio photographs received restrained exposure, colour and sharpness adjustments for the web; the original camera files are retained. Its seven project videos are original, ungraded clips. Prince Residence photographs received restrained exposure, colour and sharpness adjustments; the supplied original files are retained. Its exterior cover also received AI-assisted wire and ladder removal; the original camera image remains preserved. No Prince Residence videos were supplied. Ruchit Residence photographs received restrained exposure, colour and sharpness adjustments for the web; original HEIC files are retained. The selected exterior also received AI-assisted removal of a parked car and loose site clutter; the original remains preserved. Its 20 supplied MOV clips are presented directly without creative colour grading. The Yogesh Residence TV wall and wardrobe images received an AI-assisted colour and detail enhancement; the source images remain unchanged. TDI Wellington photographs received restrained exposure, colour and sharpness adjustments for the web; the full-frame originals are retained in the project folder. The nine TDI Wellington videos have no creative colour grading. Their camera originals are retained, with ungraded H.264 playback copies used for browser compatibility. These project assets are separate from editorial stock photography.

Brand and interactive concept

The approved DMS arch monogram represents Designs by Megha Sahni. The Essentia Salon project page uses a colour-balanced and cropped photograph of the supplied storefront image; its original is preserved in the Essentia project folder. The homepage banner remains the enhanced Babbar Residency project photograph. The homepage’s Opera Home 360° visualization is AI-generated from Opera Home project photographs: visible design features inform the scene, while unseen angles are inferred. It is not a photographed 360° tour or an exact property scan. The previous panorama and animated model remain in local backups. Three.js is MIT licensed.
