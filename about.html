A personal approach

Good design begins
with listening.

Every space has a story. Ours starts with understanding yours.

Megha Sahni founded DMS Studio in 2021 after studying interior design at Lovely Professional University. From its base in Mohali, the studio works on residential homes and commercial spaces across the Tricity.

Her process begins with how a client uses a space: daily routines, storage, movement, light and the pieces worth keeping. Those practical details inform the layout before materials and decorative decisions are made.

The portfolio brings together family homes, apartments, a football-themed children’s bedroom and commercial interiors including Essentia Salon and Sheena Nail Studio. Each case study identifies the place and details shown in the supplied project photographs, so visitors can explore the work in context.

Design and execution needs vary from one property to another. DMS Studio discusses the proposed scope, responsibilities and site requirements with each client before work proceeds.

About the studio

Who founded DMS Studio?+

DMS Studio was founded in Mohali in 2021 by Megha Sahni, who studied interior design at Lovely Professional University.

What kinds of spaces does DMS Studio design?+

The portfolio includes residential homes and apartments, children’s rooms, salons and other commercial interiors.

Where is DMS Studio based?+

The studio is based in Sunny Enclave, Mohali, and serves Chandigarh, Panchkula, Zirakpur, Kharar and New Chandigarh.

How does a project begin?+

It begins with a discussion of the property, its users, priorities and proposed scope. Site conditions and responsibilities are confirmed before detailed work proceeds.
